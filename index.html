🌱 About Us

New Beginnings, Big Ambitions

As a newly founded student collective, we believe you don't need a decades-long organization to start doing good. Every big movement begins with a small handful of passionate students taking the first step.

Pure Student-Led Drive

Built from the ground up by fellow students who want to channel their free time and energy toward impactful grassroots community service.

Action Over Words

We focus on direct, on-ground activities—stepping out together on weekends to clean shorelines, feed those in need, and plant trees.

Inclusive & Welcoming

No prior volunteering experience needed. Whether you can give two hours a month or want to help organize events, every hand counts.

🤝 Our Focus Areas

Planned Activities & Drives

Here are the meaningful social initiatives our student group is actively organizing.

🌊

Beach Cleaning Drives

Gathering student squads to clear plastic waste, litter, and debris from local beaches to safeguard our coastal marine life.

🍱

Food Donation Drives

Collecting food parcels, ration kits, and fresh meals to distribute to local shelters and people in need across our city.

🌳

Tree Plantation & Nature Care

Planting native trees in public spaces and college surroundings to encourage greener, healthier urban environments.

📚

Community Service & Outreach

Organizing book donation drives, teaching sessions, and helping hands for community welfare projects.

🎓 For Students

Why Be Part of Helping Hands?

As a student, this is a great opportunity to participate in social activities, develop teamwork and leadership skills, and make a positive impact. 🌍

Leadership & Management

Gain hands-on experience in planning drives, coordinating logistics, and leading volunteer teams.

Teamwork & Friendships

Meet like-minded students from various colleges and build meaningful bonds through shared purpose.

Real-World Impact

Experience the genuine happiness of giving back and making someone's day brighter.

Volunteer Recognition

Receive volunteer certificates and letters of appreciation for your active social contributions.
